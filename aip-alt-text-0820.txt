Figure 4: Two 3D surface plots of the 2D excited-state wave function psi_1,0 (n=1, m=0) at 6-bit coordinate resolution: real part versus position and its Fourier transform versus wavenumber, both nonzero at the origin.

Figure 5: Two 3D surface plots of the 2D excited-state wave function psi_1,1 (n=1, m=1) at 6-bit coordinate resolution: real part versus position and its Fourier transform versus wavenumber, both vanishing at the origin.

Table II: Two-column table listing the computing environment used for the simulations: CPU, memory, GPU, operating system, quantum SDK, and quantum chemistry simulator software, each with its specification or version.

Figure 6: Quantum circuit diagram of the Coulomb potential term for a 3-electron system, showing three coordinate registers and a shared ancilla register connected through repeated potential-calculation circuit blocks for each electron pair.

Figure 10: Quantum circuit diagrams for the 2D UCRZ-based simulator: (a) overall circuit dominated by the state-preparation block; (b) time-evolution circuit where the potential term uses a UCRZ circuit acting on a single ancilla qubit.

Figure 11: Six line plots comparing arithmetic and UCRZ circuit implementations across 1D, 2D, and 3D models: qubit count versus coordinate resolution (top row) and 2-qubit gate count versus coordinate resolution on a log scale (bottom row), each with an emulator resource-limit line.

Table III: Three-column table listing which circuit configurations (arithmetic-gate qubit counts, UCRZ qubit counts) were successfully run on the emulator for 1D, 2D, and 3D models.
